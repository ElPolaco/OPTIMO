Since the room renovation seems to be quite a complicated process, an approach following the divide-and-conquer algorithm appears to be natural in the given context.
Therefore, the whole task should be split into a few simpler ones.

First step - evaluation

In order to perform a well-planned renovation, it's necessary to evaluate the existing issues and their importance.
For example, removing mold from a wall should be prioritized over upgrading a well-functioning desk.
This step not only ensures that the most urgent repairs will be made, but it also helps manage the budget more wisely.

Second step - preparing the environment

Depending on the scale of the renovation, prepare a proper workspace for the job.

Third step - making repairs

It's time to work. Major tasks, like painting walls or replacing the old floor, should be done first. 

Last step - cleaning and rearranging

Every renovation produces a mess that requires a cleanup afterwards. Once clean, it's time to rearrange the old-new room.
